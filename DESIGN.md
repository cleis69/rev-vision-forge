# Design System: REV — public programme page

Scope: two worlds, kept apart. The sections from Overview to Do's and Don'ts describe the public page of a programme (`src/components/public/**`, shell in `src/components/public/app/ProgrammeApp.tsx`); it does not use the shadcn theme tokens of `src/styles.css` (`--background`, `--primary`…) and writes its own values. The marketing site of REV (`src/components/pages/**`, `src/components/rev/**`) is the second world, documented in « Marketing site world » at the end of this file, recorded from the site as shipped after the redesign of 2026-11-15; its tokens all start with `site-` (plus `stage`, `scene-screen` and `phone`) and never apply to the public page, as the public tokens never apply to it.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The sales plan that answers"**

The page is a sentence the buyer completes, « Je cherche une villa… », and the residence answers: the lots that fit stay lit on the 3D view and the list keeps only them. Everything else steps back. The ground is near-black, surfaces are raised by a few percent of white, and structure is drawn with white hairlines. The only colour that speaks is the promoter's (champagne when none is set), and it comes from data, not from the stylesheet.

It is an app, not a brochure: a fixed frame (top bar, stage, bottom action bar) with one scrolling column inside, built for a phone opened from a WhatsApp link. Density is compact; a lot is a register line, not a card. The image is never dressed up: no rounded frame, no margin, no overlay beyond the controls it needs.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black ground, white hairlines, one data-driven brand colour.
- The promoter's font carries names, the sentence and lot numbers; Inter carries the interface.
- One corner (10px) for buttons and blocks; pills only say a status.
- Phone first: a 52px top bar, the 3D view full width, a fixed action bar at the bottom.
- Lots as register lines: number, facts, status, price at the right.
- French and English everywhere. No scroll-driven effects; movement lasts 150 to 300 ms.

## Colors

Near-black and white at many strengths, with a single colour supplied by the promoter.

### Primary
- **Brand (champagne by default)** (`brand`): set at runtime as `--brand` from the promoter's data (`src/lib/brand.ts`, `src/lib/public/theme.ts`); the token value is only the fallback. It marks what is available (dot, status text, lot tag and fill on the view), fills the main action (« Planifier une visite »), and underlines the words of the sentence the buyer can change. As a tint it is used at 15% under a status pill or an open word.
- **Brand contrast** (`brand-contrast`): text on the brand colour, computed as near-black or white, whichever reads better (`--brand-contrast`). Never hard-code it.

### Secondary
- **Reserved amber** (`status-reserved`, text `status-reserved-ink`): reserved lots only, on the view (hatched), in dots, pills and status text. Also the preview banner of the promoter.
- **WhatsApp green** (`whatsapp`): the WhatsApp action only, filled with black glyph; as an outline with lighter green text (#5fe08f) where it sits beside other outlined links.

### Neutral
- **Page black** (`page`): the frame, the top bar, the bottom bar.
- **Sheet black** (`sheet`): panels that slide over the page (lot sheet, sections menu, visit form, comparison dialog).
- **Raised surface** (`surface-raised`) and **open surface** (`surface-open`): blocks inside a sheet (payment plan, simulators) and the choice opened under the sentence, inputs. Hover and current rows use white at 4 to 10%.
- **Hairlines**: `rule` between regions and around blocks, `rule-list` between lines of a list, `rule-control` around controls. Hover strengthens a control's border to 40%.
- **Ink**: `ink` for values, prices, titles; `ink-strong` for control labels; `ink-secondary` for facts and helper lines; `ink-quiet` is the floor for text. Below 50% is for chevrons, placeholders and disabled marks.
- **Selected** (`selected` / `on-selected`): a white fill with black text says "this one is chosen" in a rail or a set of choices.
- **Sold graphite** (`status-sold`): sold lots, dimmed; their number and text drop to about half strength.
- **Error** (`error-ink`): form errors, on a red tint (red at 10%, border at 30%).

### Named Rules
**The Borrowed Colour Rule.** The colour belongs to the promoter. Always write `var(--brand)` and `var(--brand-contrast)`; never a champagne hex, never a Tailwind `primary`.

**The Three Statuses Rule.** Brand is available, amber is reserved, graphite is sold, identically on the view, in the list, on the sheet and in the legend. No fourth meaning borrows one of these colours.

**The White Means Chosen Rule.** In a rail or a group of choices, the chosen item is white with black text. The brand colour is not a selection colour.

## Typography

**Display Font:** the promoter's brand font, through the `font-brand` utility (`--brand-font`; Space Grotesk when none is set; six choices in `src/lib/brand.ts`, sans and serif).
**Body Font:** Inter Variable (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui), features `cv11` and `ss01`.

**Character:** The display face is not ours to pick, so it is used at one weight (500), tight tracking, and only where the programme speaks in its own name. Inter does all the work around it, quietly, at small sizes.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, 22px / 1.42 on phones, 26px / 1.36 from 1024px, -0.01em): the sentence « Je cherche… ». Loose leading because its underlined words wrap.
- **Headline** (500, 24px, tight tracking; 30px from 640px for section titles): the programme's sections, the title of a sheet (« Lot A1 · Villa A »). Smaller steps of the same voice: 20px for a panel's key figure, 18px for a sub-title in a sheet, 16 to 18px for the programme name in the top bar.
- **Numeral** (500, 26px, line-height 1, lining and tabular figures): the lot number that opens a register line.
- **Title** (Inter 500, 15px): the label of the main action, rows of the sections menu. The price on a sheet is Inter 500 at 20px.
- **Body** (Inter 400, 14px; 16px in inputs and running text of the programme, relaxed leading): control labels, values, descriptions. 13px for the facts line of a lot and small outlined buttons.
- **Label** (Inter 400, 12px): status text, counts, helper lines, the language switch; at 500, the small group headings inside a sheet or a comparison. Always sentence case, never tracked capitals.
- **Note** (Inter 400 or 500, 11px): legal notes, the status pill, lot tags on the view.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** The brand font says names, the sentence, lot numbers and key figures. Every control, value, price and label is Inter. A price is never in the brand font.

**The Tabular Rule.** Every number that can change or stack (lot numbers, prices, counts, percentages) uses tabular figures.

## Layout

A fixed frame fills the screen (`fixed inset-0`, column): top bar, stage, bottom bar. Only the column inside scrolls; the bars never move.

- **Phone (reference, 390 wide).** Top bar 52px: logo and programme name at the left, FR / EN and the sections button at the right. Then the 3D view, full width, no margin and no radius. Then the sentence with its choices, the count row between two hairlines, the lots, the list of sections, « Propulsé par » with the REV logo. Bottom bar: the main action (48px high, filling the width) and a 48px WhatsApp square, with 10px above and the safe-area inset below.
- **From 1024px.** Top bar 64px with the sections as a rail in the middle and the two actions at the right; the bottom bar disappears. The view fills the left; the sentence and the lots sit in a fixed 440px column at the right, separated by a hairline, scrolling on its own.
- **Other sections** (programme, gallery, tours, location) use a centred container of 1152px with 20px gutters (32px from 640px) and 32 to 40px above and below, sections separated by a hairline.
- **Sheets.** The lot sheet is full width on a phone and 448px wide from 640px; its own action bar sticks to its bottom. Sheet padding is 24px.
- **Rhythm.** Gutter 20px (24px in the desktop column); 8px between sibling controls, 12px between an icon or number and its text, 16px inside a block. Touch heights: 48 (main action, inputs), 44 (secondary), 40 (choices), 36 (small controls), 32 (text-level controls); a lot line is at least 68px, a menu row 56px.
- **Motion.** State changes run 200ms on colour or opacity. The choice under the sentence fades and slides in from 4px above in 200ms; the count fades to its new value in 200ms; a line lit from the view appears in 150ms and fades over 700ms. The view's camera eases out over 500ms. Reduced motion removes all of it. Nothing is tied to scrolling, and an image does not zoom under the pointer.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth is said by tone: page black, sheet black one step up, blocks at 3 to 5% of white, and hairlines. Sheets and dialogs carry no visible shadow of their own.

The single exception is what floats on an image: controls over the 3D view, the 360° viewers and photos sit on black at 55 to 65% with a background blur, a 10 to 15% white border and a soft shadow, so they stay readable on any render.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **On-image control** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`): zoom and view rails over a view.
- **Lot tag** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`): the number tags placed on the lots of the view.

### Named Rules
**The Blur Belongs To Images Rule.** Background blur and shadow appear only on a control that sits on a photo, a render or a viewer. On the page's own black, a surface is flat with a hairline.

## Shapes

One corner, gently rounded (10px), for every button, input, choice, block and square action. A rail that wraps controls is 12px with 4px of padding, and the controls inside are 9px, so the curves stay concentric. Text-level targets (a word of the sentence, the sort, a status filter, a lot tag on the view) take a 6px corner for their hover and focus shape. Full rounding is reserved for status: the pill and the dots. Images in the frame have square corners and run edge to edge. Outside the frame, framed media and tiles that use the theme's radius steps (viewers, the map, tour and typology tiles, thumbnails) follow the scale tightened on 2026-11-15: 14px (`rounded-2xl`) and 12px (`rounded-xl`), where they were 26px and 18px.

Borders are 1px white hairlines; a dashed border means "add this" (the clauses not yet in the sentence). Focus is a 2px white ring at 70% (inset at 60% on full-width rows).

**The Pill Is A Status Rule.** If it is fully rounded, it states available, reserved or sold. Buttons, filters and tabs are never pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px), label Inter 500.
- **Primary:** brand fill, brand-contrast text, 48px high with a 18px icon at the left on the phone bars (40 to 44px in the desktop top bar and inside sections). One per bar.
- **WhatsApp:** green fill, black glyph, a 48px square beside the primary; named « WhatsApp » when it has the width.
- **Outline:** transparent, 15% white border, 85% white text, 44px (36px for small ones, 13px text); square 48px versions carry an icon alone (compare, share) in the sheet's bar.
- **Hover / Focus:** fills drop to 90% opacity, outlines go to a 40% border; 200ms; 2px white focus ring.

### Chips
- **Choice** (under the sentence, in simulators): 40px, 10px corner, 15% border; chosen is white with black text.
- **Add a clause:** 36px, dashed 25% border, a plus and the name (« Chambres », « Surface », « Budget »); turns brand while its choice is open.
- **Status filter:** text-level (32px, 6px corner), a dot, a tabular count and the status in lower case; chosen gets white at 10%, the others dim.
- **Status pill:** fully rounded, 11px 500, a 6px dot, on a 15% tint of its status colour. On a sheet, above the lot's title.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 10px.
- **Background:** white at 3% inside a sheet; white at 4% for the choice opened under the sentence.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px white at 10%.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px (14px for the open choice).
- **Disclosure blocks** (payment plan, loan, rental): a full-width row with a brand-tinted icon, a 500 label, a one-line summary at 55% and a chevron that turns; opened, a hairline then the content. Key result on a 5% white panel with the figure in the brand font.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 48px, 10px corner, white at 4%, 15% border, 16px text; label above in 14px at 80%, « (facultatif) » at 40%.
- **Focus:** border to 40% and a soft white ring.
- **Error:** border red at 70%, message in 12px light red under the field; a form-level error is a red-tinted block.
- **Range:** a native slider tinted with the brand colour, its value above in the brand font, an « OK » choice at the right.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** page black, hairline under, 52px (64px from 1024px). Programme name in the brand font, town under it in 11px.
- **Language switch:** a 10px-cornered frame with 2px padding holding FR and EN (28px high, 9px corner); the current one is white with black text.
- **Sections, phone:** an outlined 36px button opens a side sheet (max 352px) of 56px rows: 18px icon, 15px label, a brand dot on the current one. The same rows close the column under the lots.
- **Sections, desktop:** a rail (12px, white at 3%, hairline) of 36px items with 9px corners; current is white with black text.

### The sentence (signature)
A heading in the brand font that reads as a sentence: « Je cherche une villa de type A, avec 3 chambres ou plus, jusqu'à 9 000 000 MAD. » Each clause the buyer can change is brand-coloured and underlined (1px, 6px offset, the underline at 45% until hover). Touching one opens its choice right under the sentence; choosing closes it. Clauses not yet said wait under the sentence as dashed « + » buttons. Below, between two hairlines: the count (« 6 villas correspondent »), the sort, the statuses with their counts and « Effacer ».

### Register line (signature)
One lot per line, at least 68px, separated by 7% hairlines, the whole line a button. Left: the lot number in the brand font (26px, a 56px column). Middle: the facts joined by « · » (13px at 70%), then the status with its dot. Right, on the status row: the price (Inter 500, tabular), then a chevron at 35%. Sold lots lose their price and dim. The line under the pointer or lit from the view takes white at 4 to 10%.

### On-view controls
A dark translucent rail (12px corner, black at 65%, blur, 15% border) holding 36px square buttons with 9px corners (44px in the sales-office mode). Lot tags on the view are small filled labels in the status colour with the lot number in 11px semibold.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take the colour and the display font from `var(--brand)`, `var(--brand-contrast)` and `font-brand`; check a new surface with a second brand colour and a serif brand font.
- **Do** design at 390 wide first: 52px top bar, full-width view, fixed bottom bar with the main action.
- **Do** use the 10px corner for buttons and blocks, 12px for a rail around 9 to 10px controls, full rounding for statuses only.
- **Do** say a status the same way everywhere: brand, amber, graphite, each with its word.
- **Do** write every text in French and English, and give every number tabular figures.
- **Do** keep images edge to edge and untouched; put only translucent controls on them.
- **Do** keep « Propulsé par » and the REV logo on every public page.
- **Do** respect reduced motion; keep movement between 150 and 300ms on colour, opacity or a few pixels.

### Don't:
- **Don't** hard-code champagne or use the stylesheet's `primary`: the colour is the promoter's.
- **Don't** use the brand colour for a selected tab or choice; white says chosen.
- **Don't** make buttons, filters or tabs into pills.
- **Don't** put a lot in a card; a lot is a line.
- **Don't** set prices, labels or controls in the brand font.
- **Don't** tie any effect to scrolling, and don't bring the marketing site's reveals or champagne stages here.
- **Don't** set a label in tracked capitals, and don't zoom an image or a lot tag under the pointer.
- **Don't** add shadow or blur to a surface that sits on the page's black.
- **Don't** set text below 50% white.

It uses the shadcn theme of `src/styles.css`, so in code these tokens are written as theme utilities (`bg-background`, `bg-primary`, `text-primary`, `border-border/70`, `font-display`), not as literals; the `site-*` tokens are their recorded values. Nothing here applies to the public programme page above, and nothing above applies here. Subsections follow the canonical order. Pages that still depart from this world are listed under « Drift » at the end.

### Overview

**Creative North Star: "La fiche technique"**

The site shows the product at work, set on champagne, then its spec sheet line by line. Champagne is a surface as well as an accent: a « stage » carries a dark product (a black phone playing a film of the demo, a screen playing a filmed scene, the promoter space) with a soft shadow under it, and a champagne band closes every page. Everywhere else the ground is near-black, structure is hairline rules, and content sits on register lines: a name in Space Grotesk, then what it does in Inter.

A title says itself. Nothing is written above it, one phrase is champagne, and a one-sentence title drops its final period (`headline()`); two sentences keep theirs and a question keeps its mark. There is no gradient text, no glass, no glow, no tracked capitals, and nothing grows or lifts under the pointer.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black ground, rules at 70% of the border grey between lines, full-strength border above a list.
- Champagne stages (14px corners, edge to edge on phones) carrying a dark product with a soft offset shadow; a champagne band as the last part of every page.
- Space Grotesk for headings, function names and numerals; Inter for everything else; labels in sentence case.
- A heading with one champagne phrase, nothing above it, no final period on a single sentence.
- Register lines instead of card grids; the product filmed instead of a still screenshot.
- 10px corners for actions, 6px for text-level targets and tags on films; icons drawn at a 1.5 stroke.

### Colors

#### Primary
- **Champagne** (`site-champagne`, theme `--primary`; hover `site-champagne-hover`, text on it `site-on-champagne`). As a surface: the stages (offer hero, buyer scenes, promoter-space illustration, the home teaser) and the final band. As an accent: the primary action, the accented phrase of a heading, and what belongs to REV (the « Avec REV » column head, the « Nouveau » marker, step numerals, check marks and the « Vous recevez » head, register numerals on the home page, the fill that follows a film, the available status in the promoter-space illustration). 4px champagne dots at 70% separate the items of an inline list (`TrustedBy`, the tags of `Services`). A FAQ question turns champagne on hover and its plus turns champagne when open. In the header the current language is a champagne fill.
- **On champagne**: text is `site-on-champagne`; secondary text is black at 75% (`site-on-champagne-muted`), small notes black at 70%, separators black at 40%, underlines black at 30%, the outlined action's border black at 35% (`site-on-champagne-rule`, 70% on hover).

**Resolved (2026-11-17).** `--primary` and `--ring` are `oklch(0.748 0.089 84.2)`, exactly #C8A96A, the champagne of the logo files; the hover step is `oklch(0.809 0.08 84.2)`, #D9BD85. The programme pages' default brand colour follows (#c8a96a). Before, the token rendered about #C2A880, greyer than the logo.

#### Neutral
- **Ground** (`site-ground`): every part of a page outside the stages and the band; the header once scrolled. **Card** (`site-card`): the product illustration on a stage. **Elevated** (`site-elevated`): what floats inside that illustration (status menu, incoming-request notice) and the hover of a mobile menu row.
- **Ink** (`site-ink`): headings, function names, CTA labels; text links at 85%, inline-list items at 90%, the empty « — » cell at 50%, « · » separators at 25%.
- **Muted ink** (`site-ink-muted`, lightened in the redesign to about 8.5:1 on the ground): descriptions, function bodies, what the physical model and the PDF brochure do, notes, the scene titles that are not playing.
- **Rules**: `site-rule` at full strength above a list, under a table head and under each FAQ row; `site-rule-soft` (the same grey at 70%) between register lines and at the top of each part.
- **Bezel** (`site-bezel`): the 9px ring of the demo phone, on a black body.

**The Stage Rule.** Champagne is spent as a whole surface or as a precise mark, never as a thin decoration: a stage that carries the product, the closing band, the primary action, one phrase per heading, and what REV does. What the model and the brochure do stays muted grey; a neutral fact never borrows champagne.

**The Ink On Champagne Rule.** On a champagne ground everything is black: text, the filled action (`ink`: black with a champagne label), the outlined action (`inkGhost`), underlines and focus rings. White and champagne-on-champagne never appear there.

### Typography

**Display Font:** Space Grotesk Variable (`--font-display`), weight 500 only, tight tracking.
**Body Font:** Inter Variable (`--font-sans`).

- **Display** (`site-display`): the offer page's h1, three lines at 1440 wide, balanced wrap.
- **Headline** (`site-headline`): each part's h2 on the offer page. **Headline, wide** (`site-headline-wide`): the h2 of the shared headings (`SectionHeading`, `AccentHeading`, the home teaser). **Band** (`site-band`): the h2 of the final champagne band, in ink black with no accented phrase.
- **Scene** (`site-scene`, 19px): the title of a filmed scene; also the name on a two-column register (`WhyRev`, 22px from 768px). 20px (22px from 640px) for the sub-title above the comparison table; 22px (26px from 768px) for a service's name.
- **Function** (`site-function`, 17px from 640px; `site-function-compact`, 16px below): the name of a function opening a register line or a comparison row; the names of the home registers; at 15px, the programme name in the illustration.
- **Question** (`site-question`, 18px from 768px, 16px below): FAQ questions.
- **Numeral** (`site-numeral`, tabular, champagne): onboarding step numbers. Register numerals on the home page are 14px, tabular, champagne, two digits (« 01 »).
- **Lead** (`site-lead`, 15px on phones, 16px from 768px, max 62ch; up to 18px from 1024px under the shared headings): the description under a heading.
- **Item** (`site-item`, 15px 500): onboarding column heads and item titles; inline-list names.
- **Body** (`site-body`): function bodies (max 60ch), scene bodies and FAQ answers.
- **Action** (`site-action`, 14px 500): CTA labels and the tabs of the scene rail.
- **Small** (`site-small`, 13px): the stage caption and its link (500), text links, comparison cells and heads, onboarding details, the band's notes, rows of the illustration, header links.
- 12px reuses the `label` step: the « Nouveau » marker (500, champagne), the powered-by line, the small print of the illustration, the language switch, footer column heads. 12px is the floor.

**The Spec Sheet Voice Rule.** Space Grotesk names things (headings, functions, scenes, questions, numerals); Inter explains them. Labels are sentence case at 12px or more; no tracked capitals anywhere.

**The Plain Title Rule.** Nothing sits above a title: no eyebrow, no kicker, no label (the `eyebrow` prop is still accepted at call sites and never rendered). One phrase is champagne. A one-sentence title loses its final period through `headline()`.

### Layout

- **Container**: 1240px wide, gutters `site-gutter` on phones, 24px from 640px, `site-gutter-wide` from 768px.
- **Parts**: each part opens with a rule at 70%. On the offer page it runs `site-part` above and below (56px from 640px, `site-part-wide` from 1024px); on the other pages `Section` runs 56 / 64 / 96px. Heading, then the list 32px below (40px from 640px, 48px from 1024px on the home page).
- **Register line**: name in a 13rem column from 640px (15rem from 1024px), what it does beside it, an optional aside at the right from 1024px, baseline-aligned; `site-line` above and below on phones, `site-line-wide` from 640px; a rule at 70% under each line and a full-strength rule on top of the list. On phones the name sits over its body with 4px between. Home variants of the same line: a numbered register (a 2.25rem numeral column, then name and note, flowing in two columns from 1024px), a service line (name in a 17rem column, description, then an inline list of what it covers), a two-column register of name over body.
- **Inline list** (`TrustedBy`): a muted 14px label, then the names on one wrapping line separated by champagne dots, between two 70% rules. It does not move.
- **Stage**: a champagne block beside or under the text. Edge to edge on phones (it cancels the 20px gutter and has square corners), 14px corners from 640px. Offer hero and home teaser: text left, stage right (`1fr auto`, gaps 36 / 56 / 80px), the stage 64px wider than the phone on each side from 1024px, the phone 250px wide (280px from 1024px), a 13px 500 note under it; the whole stage is one link to the demo.
- **Scenes** (offer page): from 1024px, five lines in a 21rem column beside the stage (56px gap), vertically centred; below, a horizontal rail of 44px tabs above the stage and the current scene's title and body under it.
- **Comparison table**: the REV column takes the width; model and brochure columns 176px (208px from 1024px), 24px apart. On phones the table becomes lines and both answers fold into one 13px line under the function.
- **Offer hero**: top padding 96px (112px from 768px); actions full-width stacked with 10px between on phones, inline with 12px from 640px; nothing under the two actions. **Home hero**: no label and no glow; title, lead, two actions (primary and ghost), the reel beside them.
- **Two-column parts**: promoter-space lines beside a stage up to 29rem wide from 1024px (56px gap); onboarding « what you send / what you receive » in two equal columns; FAQ heading 0.8fr beside the list 1.2fr; the studio part puts its CTA at the right, bottom-aligned, over three 4:3 photos with 10px corners.

Below it, the shared pages reveal a block once as it enters the view: 0.6s, at most 16px of rise and 4px of blur (`reveal-init`; the CSS caps whatever a call site asks), with 40 to 90ms between siblings. The offer page uses no scroll reveal. Films play only in view and only for visitors who accept motion and are not saving data; with reduced motion nothing plays until a play button is pressed, and the hero phone shows its capture. CTA background 300ms with a 0.98 press; the ghost CTA's ink-at-10% fill sweeps in from the left in 350ms; a CTA's arrow moves 2px up-right on hover; a FAQ answer or a scene's body opens by grid rows in 300ms while the plus turns 45°; links and rows change colour in 200ms. Nothing zooms, lifts or floats under the pointer.

### Elevation & Depth

Flat on the ground: parts are ground, rule and type, and `surface` (the shared block utility) is a card tone with a hairline, no gradient and no shadow. Depth belongs to the product shown on a stage: a dark object on champagne with a soft shadow falling below it.

- **Phone on a stage** (`box-shadow: 0 34px 70px -28px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.75)`): the demo phone.
- **Screen on a stage** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): the scene's screen and the promoter-space illustration.
- **Popover inside the illustration** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`): its status menu and incoming-request notice.

Background blur is kept for what sits on a film or a photo: the 6px tags on videos and the play button shown over a scene's poster (black at 70%, white border at 20%).

**The Lit Product Rule.** Shadows are cast by the product (a phone, a screen, a popover of that screen), not by the page's own blocks. A block of content on the ground is never raised.

### Shapes

One corner, 10px (`block`, the theme's `--radius`), for CTAs, the header action, the play button, the illustration and its popovers, photos; 14px (`stage`) for a champagne stage from 640px; 6px (`text`) for text-level targets (text links, FAQ rows, scene tabs) and for tags on films; 8px (`site-inner`) for the small controls inside the illustration. The demo phone is `phone` with a 9px bezel ring; a scene's screen is `scene-screen` on phones and 10px on computers. The theme's larger steps were tightened to the same family: `rounded-xl` is 12px, `rounded-2xl` 14px, `rounded-3xl` 18px (they were 18 / 26 / 34px), so framed media and panels that use them now sit close to the 10px of buttons.

Full rounding is for dots (4 to 6px) and for the round sound and play glyphs that sit on a film. Focus is a 2px ring: champagne on the primary action, ink at 70% on ghost actions and text-level targets, black on champagne grounds.

**The No Pill Rule.** A labelled control (button, filter, tab) is never fully rounded on the marketing site; statuses in the illustration are a dot and a word.

### Do's and Don'ts

#### Do:
- **Do** show the product at work on a champagne stage: one dark object, a soft shadow under it, 14px corners, edge to edge on phones.
- **Do** close a page with the champagne band and use the `ink` and `inkGhost` actions on it.
- **Do** open every part with a 70% rule and let the heading speak for itself, with one champagne phrase; pass titles through `headline()`.
- **Do** put functions, services and reasons on register lines, name in Space Grotesk, explanation in Inter.
- **Do** use the 10px corner for every action; 48px for CTAs, 32px for text links.
- **Do** keep films muted, in view only, and silent under reduced motion or data saving, with a play button instead.
- **Do** write every text in French and English and keep « Propulsé par » with the REV logo on the page.

#### Don't:
- **Don't** put a label, eyebrow or kicker above a heading, and don't set labels in tracked capitals.
- **Don't** use gradient text, glass surfaces, glows, or blur on anything that is not sitting on a film or a photo.
- **Don't** lay functions out as a grid of icon cards; a card tone is for a product illustration on a stage.
- **Don't** put white or champagne text on a champagne ground; it takes black.
- **Don't** spend champagne on neutral facts or on what the model and brochure do.
- **Don't** make a labelled button, filter or tab a pill.
- **Don't** zoom, lift or float anything under the pointer, and don't lengthen reveals beyond 0.6s, 16px and 4px of blur.
- **Don't** bring these `site-*` tokens or the theme `primary` into `src/components/public/**`.
